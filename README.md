# Navier–Stokes source/formalization companion

This repository pairs the 80 numbered items and the 762 displayed equations of OpenAI's *Finite time blowup for Navier–Stokes* (166 pages) with the Lean declarations of [openai/NavierStokesAndEuler](https://github.com/openai/NavierStokesAndEuler) at commit `f9e8bc5b38b6e212696e8a30e3e91517af887bbd`, and says for each whether the Lean states what the paper states. It has three parts:

- Literature Links annotations;
- a PDF overlay viewer;
- a faithfulness report, [`REPORT.md`](REPORT.md).

## Status

These results come from a machine audit. No human has confirmed any of it. A PDE expert must review the results before any public use.

- **Items:** two independent audits, then an adjudication of the 35 items on which they differed, under one rule: an item is stated faithfully only if the paper's statement follows from stated Lean declarations by logic alone. The five medium findings that came out of that were each traced again in depth; all five were lowered to low. The 22 items ruled faithful by the adjudication or by that re-check were then attacked clause by clause; none changed.
- **The preprint's two claims:** the four medium findings in the checks of arXiv:2610.08144's claims were traced again the same way. All four were lowered to low: (8.19) and (10.19), as the paper displays them, are not what was machine-checked, but the Lean's own bounds serve every place they are used.
- **Equations:** every displayed equation was audited twice, blind, each time by a worker and a reviewer. The two runs agreed on whether the Lean has the formula for 723 of 773 blocks; the 50 they disputed were adjudicated.
- **The boxes:** the overlay was then proofread page by page against the PDF, in several rounds, and its boxes rebuilt from the fonts and the ink of the pages. They are also checked mechanically: no box edge runs through ink, every printed equation number is in the box that carries it, and no centred line of mathematics lies outside every box. Seven blocks that were not displays were dropped and seven displays that had been missed were added (and audited once).

[`REPORT.md`](REPORT.md) gives the method, the counts and every finding.

## Viewing

```sh
python3 pdf-overlay/tools/serve.py --build   # first time: publishes the viewer into site/ (no other build runs)
python3 pdf-overlay/tools/serve.py
```

Then open <http://127.0.0.1:8000/pdf-overlay/viewer/index.html>.

`serve.py` uses the PDF cached at `.lake/official-pdf/navier-stokes.pdf`. If there is no cached copy, it downloads one from the [official URL](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/32d9f210-8b73-45e0-91bc-82a30aef8a9a/navier-stokes.pdf). Either way, it checks the PDF's sha256 (`0e779481…f228f`) and serves it on loopback only. The PDF is never committed.

To check the generated files, run `python3 pdf-overlay/tools/check_generated.py`.

## Layout

| path | what it is |
|---|---|
| `annotations/<section>/[<item>/]<name>.json` | One record per (item, declaration) and per (displayed equation, declaration), plus one record with no target for an item or equation that names no declaration. A display that is the whole statement of an item, and is paired with the same declarations, has no records of its own: the item's cover it. Shared members live in `_defaults.json`. |
| `pdf-overlay/` | The viewer and its tools, generated by the formal-companion skill's `cf overlay sync` (do not edit). The exception is `overlay.config.json`. |
| `site/index.html` | The page that `build_site.py` links the viewer from. |
| `REPORT.md` | The audit report. |

## Regenerating

The annotations, the overlay and the report are generated from the audit's result file by scripts that are not in this repository. `pdf-overlay/GENERATED.json` names the formal-companion commit and the Literature Links release that generated the viewer and its tools.
